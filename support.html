Help with Panda

Finding a fresh selection

Each feed contains 20 posts. When you reach “Scroll up to refresh”, scroll back upward a little to load a fresh selection. You can also tap the message.

Two refreshes are free each day, resetting at local midnight. A monthly subscription provides limitless refreshes. You can still read the current selection and access Favourites without subscribing.

Favourites

Tap the bookmark beside a work to save it. Open Favourites to search, filter or export your collection. Favourites stay on your device and do not sync to other devices. Deleting the app can remove them, so export your list first if you want to keep a copy.

Purchases

Open the menu (⋯) in Panda’s header and choose Limitless refreshes. If your subscription is missing, select Restore purchases and use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. If you already have access, the menu item is called Subscription.

Manage or cancel your subscription in your iPhone’s Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Panda. Apple handles billing. See Apple’s cancellation instructions or request a refund from Apple.

Reading offline

The bundled artwork and text can be read offline. Opening external source pages, purchasing and restoring subscriptions need an internet connection.
