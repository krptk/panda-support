Privacy

Panda is a cultural reading app. You do not need an account to browse its collection.

Information stored on your device

Panda stores your favourites, reading position and daily free-refresh allowance on your device. This information is not sent to Panda’s publisher. There is no Panda cloud account or synchronisation service.

You can remove favourites within the app and export a text list for your own use. Deleting the app can remove its local data. Your device’s backup system may include app data; Apple’s backup settings apply.

Subscriptions

Apple processes subscription purchases and billing. Panda checks subscription access with the App Store. Panda does not receive your payment-card information. Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings. Apple’s privacy practices apply to its services.

Reading, sources and sharing

The collection is bundled with the app and can be browsed offline. Panda does not use advertising, tracking tools or third-party analytics SDKs. Opening a source link visits another website, which has its own privacy practices.

If you share a work or export favourites, the selected content is passed to the app or service you choose. Its privacy practices apply to that content.

Support and diagnostics

If you contact support, we use your email address, message and any attachments you provide to respond to your request. Support email is handled through Gmail. Please share only what is needed to explain your issue.

Support correspondence is deleted within 12 months after your issue is resolved, except records that must be retained by law. Information kept for a legal requirement is retained for the required period.

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of support correspondence by emailing the privacy contact below. We cannot retrieve your on-device favourites or reading history.

Apple may handle diagnostic information according to your device settings. During beta testing, TestFlight can make tester feedback and crash information available to the developer under Apple’s TestFlight terms and privacy practices.

This website

These information pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. The pages do not include analytics scripts, advertising or contact forms. GitHub may process technical information about visits, such as your IP address, to operate and protect its service. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Other services

Apple and Google handle information under their own privacy policies: Apple Privacy and Google Privacy Policy. Their services may process information in countries other than your own.
